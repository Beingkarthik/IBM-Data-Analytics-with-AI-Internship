# Customer Segment Analysis

## Visualization

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Purpose:
To understand the distribution of unique customers across different customer segments.

## Dataset Columns Used

- Customer_ID
- Customer_Segment

## Method

1. Grouped customers according to Customer_Segment.
2. Counted unique Customer_ID values in each segment.
3. Calculated the percentage of total unique customers represented by each segment.
4. Created a bar chart to visualize the customer segment distribution.

## Customer Segment Results

| Customer Segment | Unique Customers | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Repeat Customer | 328 | 79.04% |
| New Customer | 87 | 20.96% |
| Unknown Customer | 0 | 0.00% |

## Key Results

Highest Customer Segment: Repeat Customer

Unique Repeat Customers: 328

Percentage of Repeat Customers: 79.04%

New Customers: 87

Percentage of New Customers: 20.96%

Unknown Customers: 0

## Visualization Interpretation

Repeat Customers represent the largest customer segment, accounting for 79.04% of the unique customers.

New Customers represent 20.96% of the unique customers.

No customers were classified under the Unknown Customer segment in this analysis.

## Conclusion

The customer segment analysis shows that the majority of unique customers are classified as Repeat Customers, while a smaller proportion are classified as New Customers.
