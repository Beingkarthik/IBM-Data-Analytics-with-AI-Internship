# Top Products by Profit

## Visualization

Chart Type: Horizontal Bar Chart

Purpose:
To identify the top 10 products based on their total profit contribution.

## Dataset Columns Used

- Product
- Profit

## Method

1. Grouped the dataset by Product.
2. Calculated the total Profit for each product.
3. Sorted the products from highest to lowest profit.
4. Selected the top 10 products.
5. Created a horizontal bar chart to compare their profit.
