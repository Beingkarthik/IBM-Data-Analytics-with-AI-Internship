# Regional Revenue Analysis

## Visualization

Chart Type: Horizontal Bar Chart

Purpose:
To compare total revenue generated across different regions.

## Dataset Columns Used

- Region
- Revenue

## Method

1. Grouped the dataset by Region.
2. Calculated the total Revenue for each region.
3. Sorted the regions from highest to lowest revenue.
4. Created a horizontal bar chart to compare regional revenue.

## Regional Revenue Results

| Region | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Pune | ₹16,370,333 |
| Kolkata | ₹14,287,159 |
| Hyderabad | ₹13,267,177 |
| Chennai | ₹12,730,305 |
| Mumbai | ₹12,281,141 |
| Bangalore | ₹12,128,874 |
| Delhi | ₹12,036,840 |

## Key Results

Highest Revenue Region: Pune

Highest Revenue: ₹16,370,333

Lowest Revenue Region: Delhi

Lowest Revenue: ₹12,036,840

## Visualization Interpretation

Pune generated the highest total revenue among the regions in the dataset.

Kolkata and Hyderabad were the next highest-performing regions.

Delhi recorded the lowest total revenue among the seven regions.

## Conclusion

The regional revenue analysis shows differences in revenue performance across the seven regions. Pune was the strongest revenue-generating region, while Delhi recorded the lowest revenue.
