# Category Revenue Analysis

## Visualization

Chart Type: Bar Chart

Purpose:
To compare total revenue generated by each product category.

## Dataset Column Used

- Category
- Revenue

## Method

1. Grouped the dataset by Category.
2. Calculated the total Revenue for each category.
3. Sorted the categories from highest to lowest revenue.
4. Created a bar chart to compare category performance.

## Category Revenue Results

| Category | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Electronics | ₹59,397,987 |
| Furniture | ₹19,087,208 |
| Home | ₹8,765,755 |
| Apparel | ₹3,313,592 |
| Beauty | ₹2,842,444 |

## Key Results

Highest Revenue Category: Electronics

Highest Revenue: ₹59,397,987

Lowest Revenue Category: Beauty

Lowest Revenue: ₹2,842,444

## Visualization Interpretation

Electronics generated the highest total revenue among all categories.

Furniture generated the second-highest revenue, followed by Home, Apparel, and Beauty.

Beauty recorded the lowest total revenue among the five categories.

## Conclusion

The category analysis shows a clear difference in revenue contribution across product categories. Electronics was the strongest revenue-generating category, while Beauty was the lowest.'
