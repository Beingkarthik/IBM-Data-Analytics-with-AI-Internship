# Monthly Revenue Analysis

## Visualization

Chart Type: Line Chart

Purpose:  
To analyze monthly revenue trends and identify the highest and lowest revenue months.

## Dataset Columns Used

- `Order_Date`
- `Revenue`

## Method

1. Removed records where `Order_Date` was missing.
2. Extracted the month from `Order_Date`.
3. Grouped the data by month.
4. Calculated the total revenue for each month.
5. Arranged the months chronologically.
6. Created a line chart to visualize the monthly revenue trend.

## Key Results

- Highest Revenue Month: April
- Highest Revenue: ₹14,257,198
- Lowest Revenue Month: September
- Lowest Revenue: ₹2,671,823

## Visualization Interpretation

The monthly revenue shows noticeable variation throughout the year.

Revenue reaches its highest point in April at ₹14,257,198. After April, revenue generally declines, reaching its lowest point in September at ₹2,671,823.

Revenue then shows some recovery during October and December.

## Conclusion

The monthly revenue analysis shows that sales performance was not consistent throughout the year. April was the strongest revenue month, while September was the weakest.
