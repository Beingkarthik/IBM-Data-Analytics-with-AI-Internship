# Recommendations — AI Discovers the Story

## Recommendation 1 — Investigate and Stabilize Monthly Revenue

The business should investigate the reasons behind the large variation in monthly revenue, particularly the difference between the April peak and September low.

Possible areas to investigate include:

- Customer demand
- Promotional activity
- Product availability
- Seasonal patterns

Based on the findings, the business can plan inventory, promotions, and sales activities more effectively during lower-performing periods.

## Recommendation 2 — Strengthen the Electronics Category

Electronics generated the highest category revenue at ₹59,397,987.

The business should analyze the products, pricing, demand, and sales volume within Electronics to understand what is driving its strong performance.

If further analysis confirms sustained demand, the business could consider maintaining appropriate inventory levels and supporting the category with targeted sales activities.

## Recommendation 3 — Focus on Repeat Customer Retention

Repeat Customers represent 79.04% of the unique customer base.

The business should continue focusing on customer retention while also investigating the factors that encourage customers to make repeat purchases.

Possible actions include:

- Monitoring repeat purchase behavior
- Identifying high-value repeat customers
- Improving customer engagement
- Developing appropriate retention strategies

These actions should be evaluated using additional customer behavior data.
