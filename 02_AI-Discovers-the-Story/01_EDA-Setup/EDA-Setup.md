# EDA Setup — AI Discovers the Story

## Masterclass
Masterclass 2 — AI Discovers the Story

## Analysis Stage
Stage 3 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Objective
The objective of this stage is to explore the cleaned e-commerce dataset, identify patterns, and convert the analysis into meaningful business insights.

## Tools
- Google Sheets
- Google Colab
- Gemini
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

## Dataset
The cleaned e-commerce dataset prepared during Masterclass 1 is used as the input for Exploratory Data Analysis.

## EDA Workflow

Business Question
        ↓
Ask Gemini
        ↓
Generate Analysis Code
        ↓
Run Code in Google Colab
        ↓
Inspect Results
        ↓
Interpret Results
        ↓
Business Insight

## Analysis Areas

### Sales Analysis
- Monthly Revenue
- Sales Trends
- Category Performance
- Top Products
- Bottom Products

### Customer Analysis
- Total Customers
- Repeat vs One-Time Customers
- High-Value Customers
- Customer Behavior Segments

### Regional Analysis
- Regional Revenue
- Best Performing Region
- Lowest Performing Region
- High-Growth Regions
- Underserved Regions

## Important Principle

AI-generated results must be validated before accepting them.

The workflow is:

AI Suggestion → Check Numbers → Verify → Accept/Reject → Business Insight

AI-generated claims should not be treated as facts unless they are supported by the dataset.
