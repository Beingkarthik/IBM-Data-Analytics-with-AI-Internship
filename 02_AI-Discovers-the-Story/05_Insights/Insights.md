# Insights — AI Discovers the Story

## Insight 1 — Revenue Performance Is Highly Variable

The monthly revenue pattern shows substantial variation during the year. Revenue reached its highest level in April at ₹14,257,198 and its lowest level in September at ₹2,671,823.

This indicates that overall sales performance is uneven across the year, with a strong peak followed by a significant decline.

---

## Insight 2 — Electronics Is the Dominant Revenue Category

Electronics generated ₹59,397,987 in revenue, which was substantially higher than the other categories.

The next highest category, Furniture, generated ₹19,087,208.

This indicates that Electronics is the strongest contributor to overall category revenue in the dataset.

---

## Insight 3 — The Customer Base Is Predominantly Repeat Customers

Repeat Customers account for 328 unique customers, representing 79.04% of the identified customer base.

New Customers account for 87 unique customers, representing 20.96%.

This indicates that repeat customers form the majority of the customer base analyzed.

---

## Insight 4 — Pune Is the Highest Revenue-Generating Region

Pune generated the highest regional revenue at ₹16,370,333, while Delhi generated the lowest at ₹12,036,840.

This indicates that revenue performance differs across regions, with Pune currently leading the regional comparison.

---

## Insight 5 — Laptop Leads Product-Level Profit

Laptop generated the highest total profit among the analyzed products at ₹2,180,953, followed by Camera at ₹1,883,293.

This indicates that Laptop is currently the strongest profit-generating product in the dataset, while profit contribution varies considerably across products.
