## Business Insights

Branch C (Mumbai) is the strongest-performing branch based on total sales and average transaction value.

Beverages is the highest-selling category in both sales value and quantity sold, making it important for inventory planning.

Members generate substantially higher total sales than Normal customers, indicating the importance of maintaining customer membership and engagement.

UPI is the most-used payment method and also generates the highest payment-method sales.

Cheese generates the highest product-level sales, while Potato has the highest quantity sold.

July 2026 has low recorded sales because the dataset contains transactions only through July 1, so it should not be interpreted as a complete-month decline.
