# Churn Definition — AI Predicts What Happens Next

## Objective

Before building the prediction model, a clear definition of customer churn is required.

For this project, the churn target is represented by the `Churn_Status` column.

## Churn Status

The customer-level dataset uses:

| Churn_Status | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Not Churned |
| 1 | Churned |

The churn target is used as the outcome that the machine learning model attempts to predict.

## Churn Definition Used

For the classroom exercise, the churn definition used in the analysis was based on customer behavior and the selected churn criteria.

The resulting `Churn_Status` variable was then used as the target variable for the prediction model.
