# Business Questions

## 1. Objective

The objective is to identify meaningful business questions that can be answered using the cleaned e-commerce dataset.

The questions are organized into four areas:

- Sales
- Product
- Customer
- Region

## 2. Sales Questions

### Q1. Which months generate the highest total revenue?

Relevant columns:

- Order_Date
- Revenue
- Month
- Year

### Q2. Which months generate the highest total profit?

Relevant columns:

- Order_Date
- Profit
- Month
- Year

### Q3. Is revenue increasing or decreasing over time?

Relevant columns:

- Order_Date
- Revenue
- Month
- Year

## 3. Product Questions

### Q4. Which products generate the highest revenue?

Relevant columns:

- Product
- Revenue

### Q5. Which products generate the highest profit?

Relevant columns:

- Product
- Profit

### Q6. Which product categories are underperforming?

Relevant columns:

- Category
- Revenue
- Profit

## 4. Customer Questions

### Q7. Who are the highest-value customers based on revenue?

Relevant columns:

- Customer_ID
- Revenue

### Q8. Which customers generate the highest profit?

Relevant columns:

- Customer_ID
- Profit

### Q9. What proportion of customers are new versus repeat customers?

Relevant columns:

- Customer_ID
- Customer_Segment

## 5. Region Questions

### Q10. Which regions generate the highest revenue?

Relevant columns:

- Region
- Revenue

### Q11. Which regions generate the highest profit?

Relevant columns:

- Region
- Profit

### Q12. Which regions have the lowest sales performance?

Relevant columns:

- Region
- Revenue
- Profit

## 6. Priority Questions

Based on business impact, the following five questions are prioritized:

1. Which months generate the highest total revenue?
2. Which products generate the highest profit?
3. Which product categories are underperforming?
4. Who are the highest-value customers based on revenue?
5. Which regions generate the highest profit?

These questions cover Sales, Product, Customer, and Region rather than focusing on only one business area.

## 7. Why These Questions Matter

Answering these questions can help management understand:

- Revenue trends
- Profit performance
- Product performance
- Customer value
- Regional performance

The answers can support future business decisions based on the cleaned dataset.

## 8. Masterclass 1 Principle

Good business questions should be:

- Specific
- Measurable
- Tied to a decision
- Prioritized by business impact

The business question should come before the analysis.
